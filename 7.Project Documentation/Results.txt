RESULTS

The LegalEase application provides a functional web interface for AI-assisted legal document generation.

1. The interface collects document type, parties, terms and effective date.
2. Requests are validated by Pydantic and processed by the FastAPI /generate endpoint.
3. Gemini generates a structured legal document (sample: Rental Agreement between Ramesh (Landlord) and Suresh (Tenant), effective 25-09-2026).
4. The document is displayed in a styled preview with clause-wise structure.
5. Users can edit the generated text before exporting.
6. The document can be downloaded as TXT, DOCX (Times New Roman, centered title, footer) and PDF (headings, bulleted terms, footer).
7. Errors are returned as HTTP 500 with a readable message.
8. Generated documents are AI drafts and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before real use.
